# Discovery — First Meeting (one page)

Full list: [discovery-questions.md](discovery-questions.md) — use it for the
follow-up sessions with IT, finance, and the reps.

## Ask him to bring

Five real quotes — the two most complicated, and one that went wrong.
Plus the Excel files with formulas intact.

## Pricing — decides the project

1. Where does the price of a line come from? Trace one, concretely.
2. Do different customers pay different prices for the same product? On what basis?
3. Are there volume breaks? Per line, per order, or per year?
4. Is any price *calculated* rather than looked up? Show me the formula.
5. Who can discount, up to what, and what happens above that?
6. **Is there any pricing rule that's currently in someone's head?**
   Ask it exactly like that, then wait through the silence.

## Current process

7. Walk me through your last quote, from the customer's email to the order.
8. Now the last *difficult* one. What made it difficult?
9. What gets re-typed after a quote is accepted, and into what?

## Integration

10. **What system does an accepted quote need to reach, and does it have an API?**
    The single biggest threat to the estimate. If he doesn't know, get whoever
    owns the ERP into the next meeting.

## Products

11. How many products, and do they have variants?
12. Are all products in the catalog, or do reps add free-text lines? What proportion?

## Documents

13. What must appear on the quote for legal or contractual reasons?
14. Which Incoterms, and do you produce a proforma invoice today?
15. Which currencies? Where does the rate come from, and who absorbs the movement
    if it shifts before acceptance?

## The manager himself

16. "Improve sales" — **more quotes, faster turnaround, higher win rate, or better
    margin? Rank them.** They pull in different directions.
17. Name three tasks you want to stop happening by hand.
18. Discounts *blocked* by the system, or warned only? (Blocking changes the
    architecture.)
19. Will using this be mandatory, enforced by whom, starting when?
20. **Six months from now, how do you know this was worth building?** Get a number.

---

## Two things that aren't questions

**Watch a rep build a real quote, and time it.** That number is the Phase 1
success metric. The manager is the sponsor, not the daily user — if the system
is slower than Excel for the reps, they will keep using Excel.

**If you hear "we'll figure out the pricing later" — stop.** Pricing is the system.
